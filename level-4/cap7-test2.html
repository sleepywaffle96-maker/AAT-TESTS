1. Under advanced treasury monitoring, an accountant applies the Baumol model to balance cash accounts. What is the fundamental operational objective of this specific formula?
Solution Note: The Baumol model treats cash balances like an inventory problem. It optimizes cash levels by mathematical trade-offs between holding cash (lost investment interest) versus transforming securities into liquid funds (fixed broker fees).

3. Which corporate debt instrument represents an unsecured, short-term commercial paper issued by large creditworthy companies directly to institutional investors to raise immediate cash?
Solution Note: Commercial Paper provides highly rated large corporations with a shortcut to cheap liquidity. Because it is issued directly to wholesale money markets without bank intermediation, borrowing costs are kept extremely low.

Solution Note: Cash sweeping is an automated netting tool. It daily harvests idle liquidity from dispersed subsidiaries and concentrates it into a single corporate treasury ledger to maximize earning power or clear group overdraft exposures.

11. A corporation opts for Debt Factoring instead of standard Invoice Discounting to manage its ledger collection delays. What structural operational parameter shifts under this choice?
Solution Note: Debt Factoring is an outsourced solution. Unlike confidential invoice discounting where the company collects its own debts, a factor takes over sales ledger maintenance and pursues clients directly.

Solution Note: Capital rationing occurs when funds are restricted. Project selection shifts from simple NPV rankings to using the Profitability Index (NPV / Initial Outlay) to optimize the capped capital pool.

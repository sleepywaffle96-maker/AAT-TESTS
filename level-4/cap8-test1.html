11. Under corporate insolvency acts, what represents the primary distinction between a company entering Administration versus going into compulsory Liquidation?
Solution Note: Administration acts as an emergency rescue attempt, shielding the business from creditor litigation to restructure operations. Liquidation is terminal, dissolving the corporate entity entirely.

Solution Note: The Debt Proportionality Trap warns that collection outlays must never outstrip the debt's value. Spending hundreds of pounds in administration to chase a £50 balance violates cost-benefit rules.

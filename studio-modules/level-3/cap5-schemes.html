Scheme 5.1: The 5 Categories of Ethical Threats & Official Examples

1. Self-Interest Threat

Financial or other interests inappropriately influence the accountant's judgment.

⚠️ Example: Holding shares in a client's firm or dependency on a massive profit bonus.

2. Self-Review Threat

An accountant will not appropriately evaluate the results of a previous judgment or service performed by themselves.

⚠️ Example: Auditing or verifying final accounts that you prepared.

3. Advocacy Threat

Promoting a client's or employer's position to the point that objectivity is compromised.

⚠️ Example: Acting as an advocate for an audit client in a legal dispute.

4. Familiarity Threat

Due to a long or close relationship, an accountant will be too sympathetic to client interests or too accepting of entries.

⚠️ Example: Auditing a close relative's corporate financial ledger records.

5. Intimidation Threat

Being deterred from acting objectively because of actual or perceived commercial pressures or undue influence.

⚠️ Example: Being threatened with immediate contract dismissal or career damage.

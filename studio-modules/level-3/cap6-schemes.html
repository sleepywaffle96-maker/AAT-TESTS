Scheme 6.1: Legal Business Structures Comparison

Sole Traders

Owned by one individual. Total control but absolute risk exposure.

⚠️ Unlimited Personal Liability: Personal assets can be legally seized to settle business deficits.

Ordinary Partnerships

Two or more partners operating under a joint Partnership Agreement structure.

⚠️ Joint and Several Liability: Every partner is personally liable for all partnership debts.

Limited Companies (Ltd/Plc)

Separate legal entities regulated strictly by Companies House and Corporation Tax mandates.

🛡️ Limited Liability: Shareholder loss is strictly capped at the nominal value of their share investment.

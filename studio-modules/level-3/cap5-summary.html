AAT LEVEL 3 RECAP

Chapter 5: Professional Ethics Quick Cheat Sheet

🎯 CORE ETHICAL TAXONOMY MEMORY BLOCK:

• 5 Principles: Integrity, Objectivity, Competence & Due Care, Confidentiality, Professional Behaviour.
• 5 Threats: Self-Interest, Self-Review, Advocacy, Familiarity, Intimidation.
• AML Offenses: Failure to Disclose (Max 5 Years) | Tipping Off (Max 5 Years).

1. Anti-Money Laundering Regulations (POCA)

  • Gatekeeper Duty: Accountants operate within the "regulated sector" and must identify clients (CDD) using authoritative documentation (passports, registries) before executing any trade agreements.
  • Suspicion Pipeline: Internal team alerts must be centralized through the appointed MLRO, who handles the submission of formal Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) directly to the National Crime Agency (NCA).
  • Legal Sanction Limits: Hiding evidence of laundering or failing to disclose a suspicion carries up to 5 years imprisonment. Alerting a suspect of an active SAR audit (Tipping Off) carries an identical 5-year criminal sentence.

2. Corporate Data Safety & Public Disclosures

  • DPA / GDPR Compliance: Commercial accounting databases must capture and process private records lawfully, transparently, securely against hacks, and keep data only for specified legal compliance timelines.
  • Bribery Act Controls: Criminalizes the acts of giving, receiving, or failing to prevent a commercial bribe within corporate management hierarchies, enforcing strict organizational liability.
  • PIDA Whistleblowing: Provides comprehensive employment protection against retaliation if an accountant must bypass compromised internal boards to report illegalities to public oversight bodies.
⚠️ ADVANCED EXAM TRAPS RECAP (INVISIBLE ERRORS):
  • The Inadvertent Clarification Trap: Never confront a client or ask for details after identifying clear fraud lines. Any change in behavior that alerts the target constitutes criminal Tipping Off.
  • The Privilege Illusion: Client confidentiality is never absolute. The principle is completely overridden by legal obligations under POCA; data cannot be withheld from HMRC or the NCA.
  • The Safeguard Flaw: Automated controls are not safeguards if the threat remains unaddressed. Safeguards must actively eliminate or minimize risk. If a threat cannot be mitigated, the member must **decline or resign**.

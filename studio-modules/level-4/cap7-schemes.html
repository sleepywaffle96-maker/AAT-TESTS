Scheme 7.1: The Treasury Allocation Triangle Variables

1. Security (Capital Protection)

Absolute mitigation of default risk elements when placing corporate financial balances.

🛡️ Focus: Low-risk assets like Treasury Bills backed by sovereign governments.

2. Liquidity (Speed of Recovery)

The conversion velocity of investment assets back into active bank checking ledger metrics.

📌 Focus: Overnight deposit structures or tradable Certificates of Deposit (CDs).

3. Yield (Interest Return)

The monetary scaling profit generated by temporary investments of corporate surpluses.

⚠️ Trade-off: Higher yield options inherently introduce higher liquidity locks or capital risks.

Scheme 7.3: Surplus Instruments & Deficit Funding Frameworks

Financial Instrument Treasury Operations Class Strategic Cash Management Role
Treasury Bills (T-Bills) Short-Term Sovereign Debt Offers absolute security baseline and high liquidity. Issued at a discount to par value.

Bank Overdraft Flexible Deficit Facility Resolves sudden temporary cash deficits. Highly flexible but applies expensive daily interest fees.

Chapter 8: Credit Management Quick Cheat Sheet

🎯 CRMN LIQUIDITY & COLLECTIONS RECAP BLOCK:

• 5 Cs Frame: Character, Capacity, Capital, Collateral, Conditions.
• DSO Formula: (Trade Receivables / Gross Credit Sales) × 365 [Omit Cash Sales].
• Factoring Splits: With Recourse (Firm retains risk) | Without Recourse (Factor bears bad debts).

1. Risk Mappings & Aged Ledger Monitoring

  • Assessment Metrics: Credit Scoring applications evaluate financial statements cells, Companies House public registries, and external database ratings before setting credit boundaries.
  • Aged Debtors Ledgers: Tracks credit defaults risk by segmenting general accounts receivable into chronological delay rows (Current, 30, 60, 90+ days).
  • DSO Fluctuations: High Days Sales Outstanding trajectories warn that vital cash assets are trapped, multiplying overdraft variable interest expenses.

2. Financing Liquidity & Legal Remedial Paths

  • Invoice Discounting: Draws private short-term cash loans using ledger totals as collateral security, maintaining internal management confidentiality lines.
  • Progressive Escalation Path: Credit departments enforce statements alerts, telephone chasing, final Letter Before Action (LBA) before launching litigation.
  • Legal Executions: Deploys County Court processes, late payment statutory commercial interest surcharges, or insolvency winding-up actions for severe corporate balances defaults.
⚠️ ADVANCED EXAM TRAPS RECAP (INVISIBLE ERRORS):
  • The Recourse Risk Illusion: Assuming that selling invoices via "With Recourse" factoring deletes bad debt exposures. The factor will recharge uncollectible defaults back to the firm.
  • The DSO Denominator Total Revenue Error: Integrated cash sales entries inside debtor days equations. Cash receipts have zero link to trade receivables and skew DSO velocity maps.
  • The Premature Litigation Fault: Filing instant court claims on day one of a payment delay. Case studies penalize non-compliance with progressive collection pathways rules.

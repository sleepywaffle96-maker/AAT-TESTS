Chapter 2: Financial Statements Quick Cheat Sheet

🎯 DAIF REGULATORY & PERFORMANCE MEMORY BLOCK:

• IAS 38 Rules: Research = Expense immediately | Development = Capitalize asset if viable.
• ROCE Formula: Operating Profit / (Total Equity + Long-Term Debt) * 100.
• Gearing Split: Interest-Bearing Debt / (Total Equity + Interest-Bearing Debt) * 100.

1. Standards Implementation (IAS/IFRS Framework)

  • IAS 16 Property, Plant, Equipment: Asset fair value revaluations must bypass the P&L and transfer directly into the Statement of Changes in Equity (SOCIE) under Revaluation Reserve.
  • IAS 37 Provisions and Risks: Provisions require a present obligation arising from past events, where an outflow of cash resources is probable and reliably estimable.
  • IAS 7 Statement of Cash Flows: Segregates transactional cells into Operating flows (starting from Operating Profit), Investing flows (PPE purchases/sales), and Financing flows (shares and bank loan adjustments).

2. Financial Diagnostics & Ratio Metrics

  • Profitability & Efficiency: Asset Turnover (Revenue / Capital Employed) evaluates sales generation volume relative to total funding bases.
  • Liquidity & Solvency: Acid Test (Current Assets minus Inventory divided by Current Liabilities) strips less liquid cell structures to extract exact short-term safety parameters.
  • Long-Term Debt Exposure: Gearing monitors long-term risk. Interest Cover maps the net capacity to clear ongoing finance costs out of operating profit cells.
⚠️ ADVANCED EXAM TRAPS RECAP (INVISIBLE ERRORS):
  • The Research Capitalization Fault: Capitalized testing costs under IAS 38. Research lines can never build asset balances and must clear through the P&L immediately.
  • The Gearing Denominator Split Error: Forgot to add long-term loans inside the denominator pool. Gearing relies strictly on the total capital employed base.
  • The SOCIE Bonus Issue Illusion: Logged a bonus share issue as a liquid transaction inside the Statement of Cash Flows. Bonus issues capitalize existing reserves with zero net cash generation.

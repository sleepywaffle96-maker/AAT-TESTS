AAT LEVEL 4 RECAP

Chapter 4: Business Tax Quick Cheat Sheet

🎯 BSTX TAXABLE ADJUSTMENTS BLOCK:

• Disallowable: Depreciation, Customer Entertaining, Capital Assets Outlays (Add Back).
• Capital Allowances: AIA (100%), Main Pool WDA (18%), Special Rate Pool WDA (6%).
• Loss Options: Current Year, Carry Back (12 Months), Carry Forward (Same Trade).

1. Profit Adjustments & Capital Allowances

  • Profit Conversions: Non-trade income (rental, interest) must be removed from trading accounts and reassessed in its specific statutory schedule.
  • HMRC Pooling Mechanics: AIA provides immediate write-offs. Balance values in the Main Pool receive an 18% WDA, while the Special Rate Pool handles building integral assets at 6%.
  • Balancing Adjustments: A Balancing Charge occurs if disposal proceeds exceed the remaining pool WDV, while a Balancing Allowance triggers to clear terminated single-asset pools.

2. Chargeable Gains & Losses Treatment

  • Corporate Capital Gains: Merged directly into the corporation tax computation pool. Indexation allowance applies to freeze historical values up to December 2017.
  • Loss Restrictions: Capital losses face strict fences. They can only offset current or future capital gains and have zero interaction with trading profits.
  • Loss Claims: Carry Back claims generate quick cash refunds against the past year's total profits. Carry Forward claims securely offset future trade margins.
⚠️ ADVANCED EXAM TRAPS RECAP (INVISIBLE ERRORS):
  • The Depreciation Add-Back Slip: Left accounting depreciation inside net tax calculations. It must always be added back before calculating Capital Allowances.
  • The Entertainment Split Mistake: Allowed customer entertainment costs to reduce taxable income. Employee entertaining is allowable; customer entertaining is strictly disallowable.
  • The Loss Offset Overreach: Attempted to use unabsorbed capital losses against trading or rental profits. Capital losses can only carry forward to reduce future chargeable capital gains.

Scheme 8.1: The 5 Cs of Credit Assessment Risk Matrix

⚠️ Audit: Adjust internal credit rating caps if market inflation or interest rates rise.

Scheme 8.3: Receivables Efficiency Ratio Equations

Credit Control Metric Statutory Accounting Formula Strategic Cash Flow Diagnostic Objective
Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) (Trade Receivables / Gross Credit Sales) * 365 Tracks ledger capital locks velocity. Denominator MUST exclude all raw cash sales lines.
Collection Efficiency % (Actual Cash Collections / Target Overdue Debt) * 100 Measures team collections efficacy against targeted chronological overdue debtor ledger cells.
Factoring Without Recourse Ledger sold + Factor absorbs risk Sells ledger for immediate cash flow. Factor absorbs 100% of future bad debt loss write-offs.
Factoring With Recourse Ledger sold + Corporate absorbs risk Sells ledger but company retains ultimate default risk. Factor charges unpaid debts back to firm.

AAT LEVEL 4 RECAP

Chapter 5: Personal Tax Quick Cheat Sheet

🎯 PLTX TAXATION BALANCES BLOCK:

• Pipeline order: Non-Savings (1st) -> Savings (2nd) -> Dividends (3rd).
• Taper rule: If ANI > £100,000, reduce Personal Allowance by £1 for every £2 of excess.
• NIC Classes: Class 1 (Employees/Employers), Class 2 (Flat self-employed), Class 4 (% self-employed).

1. Income Tax Pipeline & Benefit-in-Kind Calculations

  • Schedules Separation: Personal income maps strictly across individual non-savings, savings, and dividend bands. Basic, higher, and additional thresholds scale sequentially.
  • The Personal Allowance vanishing boundary: If Adjusted Net Income hits £125,140, the tax-free Personal Allowance threshold reaches zero.
  • P11D Company Car evaluation: Car perks are calculated by taking the manufacturer's official list price and multiplying it by the statutory percentage defined by the vehicle's CO2 g/km emissions.
  • National Insurance splits: Class 1 Primary handles workforce PAYE scomputi, while Class 4 tracks self-employed adjusted net business profits.

2. Capital Gains Tax & Inheritance Tax Baselines

  • Individual Capital Gains: Managed separately from regular income tax. Individuals apply the Annual Exempt Amount (AEA) to net profits and load balances onto progressive CGT scaling brackets.
  • Residential Property Surcharge: Capital gains derived from secondary domestic residences face higher statutory percentage bands.
  • Inheritance Tax Thresholds: The Nil Rate Band (NRB) shields estate asset values up to £325,000 at a 0% rate. Lifetime gifts to individuals qualify as Potentially Exempt Transfers (PETs), becoming entirely tax-free after 7 years.
⚠️ ADVANCED EXAM TRAPS RECAP (INVISIBLE ERRORS):
  • The Personal Allowance Taper Failure: Loaded standard allowance deductions on individuals whose Adjusted Net Income exceeds £100,000 without applying the £1 for every £2 taper reduction.
  • The Corporate Vehicle Discount Illusion: Utilized the actual corporate net purchase cost for car benefit metrics. HMRC guidelines command the strict application of the official manufacturer's list price.
  • The Dividend Band Consumption Slip: Ignored the dividend tax-free allowance when calculating band availability. The dividend allowance consumes space within the basic rate band, pushing subsequent income into higher brackets.

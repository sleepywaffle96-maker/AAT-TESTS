Scheme 4.1: Net Accounting Profit to Taxable Trading Profit Adjustments

Disallowable Expenses (+ Add Back)

Costs listed in P&L cells that are explicitly banned by UK statutory tax rules.

⚠️ Examples: Accounting Depreciation, Customer Entertaining, Capital Assets outlays.

Non-Trading Income (- Deduct)

Revenue lines embedded inside accounting profits that belong to distinct tax schedules.

📌 Examples: Bank Interest received, Commercial Rental income, Dividends streams.

Capital Allowances (- Deduct)

HMRC-approved statutory relief system that replaces accounting depreciation allocations.

🛡️ Controls: AIA 100% immediate deductions, Main Pool WDA deductions.

Scheme 4.3: Corporate Loss Relief Allocation Strategies

Loss Relief Allocation Statutory Execution Window Strategic Tax Shield Operations
Current Year Claim Identical Accounting Period Offsets trading loss directly against current non-trading income or corporate chargeable gains.
Carry Back Relief Preceding 12-Month Period Offsets against total taxable profits of the past year to secure an immediate cash tax refund from HMRC.
Carry Forward Relief Future Accounting Periods Unabsorbed losses advance automatically to reduce trading profit cells of the identical trade line.
Capital Loss Restriction Capital Gains Only Banned from reducing general trading income. Can only carry forward to net future chargeable capital gains.

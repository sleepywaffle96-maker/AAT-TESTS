Scheme 2.2: IAS 7 Statement of Cash Flows Architecture

======================================================================================== STATUTORY LIQUIDITY SEPARATION ENGINE ======================================================================================== [OPERATING ACTIVITIES SECTION] • Starts from Operating Profit. Adjusts out non-cash adjustments (Depreciation, Provisions). • Tracks Working Capital shifts: Inventory variations, Receivables/Payables cells changes. ↓ [INVESTING ACTIVITIES SECTION] • Captures non-current assets transactions. Cash inflows from selling equipment. • Cash outflows spent buying long-term intangibles or plant property (PPE). ↓ [FINANCING ACTIVITIES SECTION] • Tracks structural funding shifts. Cash inflows from issuing new share capital stakes. • Cash outflows paid out to settle long-term bank loans or finance lease lines. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- CRITICAL EXAM BOUNDARY: Dividend payments can sit in Operating or Financing flows. BONUS SHARES TRAP: Zero impact on this sheet. SOCIE capitalization shift only. ========================================================================================

Scheme 2.3: Core Ratio Equations Framework

Financial Ratio Vector Statutory Mathematical Formula Strategic Accounting Diagnostic Core
ROCE % (Operating Profit / Capital Employed) * 100 Measures asset efficiency. Capital Employed = Equity + Long-Term Liabilities.
Quick Ratio (Acid Test) (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities Isolates baseline short-term solvency by excluding less liquid inventory cells.
Gearing Ratio % (Long-Term Debt / Total Capital Employed) * 100 Measures long-term debt risk exposure. Denominator must include both debt and equity.
Interest Cover Operating Profit / Finance Costs Interest Expense Tracks capacity to pay debt charges. High ratio indicates strong security shields.

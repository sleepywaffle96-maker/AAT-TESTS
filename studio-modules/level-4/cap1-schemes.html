Scheme 1.1: Budgetary Systems & Strategic Allocation

Incremental Budgeting

Adjusts historical figures by a percentage factor (inflation or expected growth metrics).

⚠️ Risk: Carries forward historical waste and systemic budgeting slack.

Zero-Based Budgeting (ZBB)

Builds the budget framework from zero. Every entry must be fully justified by department managers.

🛡️ Advantage: Eliminates slack and optimizes operational resource allocation pools.

Rolling Budgets

Continuous forecasting system adding a new period immediately as the current month or quarter expires.

📈 Focus: Maintains precision in highly volatile trading environments.

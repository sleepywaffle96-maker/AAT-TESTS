Chapter 7: Cash and Treasury Quick Cheat Sheet

🎯 CTMN LIQUIDITY & FORECASTING RECAP BLOCK:

• Treasury Triangle: Security (Capital) <-> Liquidity (Speed) <-> Yield (Return).
• Receipts Pipeline: Gross Sales Base -> Subtract Bad Debt % FIRST -> Apply Monthly Lag %.
• Absolute Omissions: Depreciation, Provisions, Amortization = Zero entry in columns.

1. Cash Budgeting & Lead-Lag Adjustments

  • Value Convention Rules: Cash forecasts monitor absolute physical bank transactions. All sales and purchases entries must be loaded using GROSS values (including VAT).
  • Timing Isolations: Heavy statutory tax settlements (VAT quarterly, Corporation Tax) and structural equipment cash transactions must map strictly to their target calendar date cells.
  • Centralized Pooling Advantages: PULLing individual divisional bank profiles into a singular corporate centralized pool optimizes baseline cash weights and reduces loan costs.

2. Surplus Investments & Funding Deficits

  • Sovereign Protections: Treasury Bills (T-Bills) provide absolute capital safety backed by governments, issued at a standard discount baseline.
  • Divisional Liquidity Autonomy: Certificates of Deposit (CDs) generate competitive yields while providing active secondary trading market exit paths.
  • Deficit Coverage Mechanics: Unplanned liquidity gaps deploy commercial bank overdrafts (flexible short-term daily interest scomputi) or committed Revolving Credit Facilities (RCF).
⚠️ ADVANCED EXAM TRAPS RECAP (INVISIBLE ERRORS):
  • The Depreciation Schedulation Fault: Included book accounting depreciation inside the payments track. Depreciation is a non-cash adjustments element with zero cash drain.
  • The Premature Lag Distribution Slip: Distributed full sales figures across subsequent months before extracting uncollectible bad debts. Bad debts must clear the pool **before** applying lag percentages.
  • The Net Scheme Value Error: Keyed in net invoice figures inside standard cash forecasts. Cash budgets track bank balance lines, forcing the deployment of **gross amounts (including VAT)**.

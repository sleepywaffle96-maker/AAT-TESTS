Scheme 3.1: The Three Layers of Internal Controls

1. Preventative Controls

Designed to block transactions anomalies or active fraud before execution loops take place.

🛡️ Core Metric: Segregation of Duties (SoD) and restricted access permissions.

2. Detective Controls

Designed to identify errors or operational gaps immediately after transaction cycles occur.

📌 Core Metric: Independent ledger reconciliations and physical counts audits.

3. Corrective Controls

Designed to resolve Exceptions and restore infrastructure status after gaps trigger anomalies alerts.

⚠️ Core Metric: Error correction protocols and business continuity backups plans.

Chapter 1: Applied Management Accounting Quick Cheat Sheet

🎯 AMA OPERATIONAL MATHEMATICAL RECAP BLOCK:

• Variance Decomposition: Total Variance = Planning Split + Operational Split.
• Limiting Factor Formula: Contribution per unit / Required scarce resources amount.
• Transfer Price Bounds: Minimum (Incremental + Opportunity Cost) | Maximum (Lower of Market or Net divisional revenue).

1. Advanced Budgetary Methods & Flexible Tracking

  • Budget Types: ZBB rejects benchmarks and forces complete justification from base zero. Continuous/Rolling updates remain live by adding extensions as months/quarters close.
  • The Flexing Baseline Rule: Recompute initial cost benchmarks using the actual volume parameters achieved. Variable elements change proportionally; fixed elements stay completely constant.
  • Variance Extraction Splits: Planning components measure unavoidable structural tracking updates (treated as error revisals). Operational components isolate internal shop floor manufacturing efficiency and waste.

2. Short-Term Scarcity & Divisional Pricing

  • Bottleneck Optimization: Rank alternatives by contribution per unit of bottleneck consumption. Do not allocate restricted lines by pure unit margin.
  • Transfer Pricing Goal Congruence: Internal transfer pricing nodes must allow independent divisional leaders to automatically maximize general corporate cash flows.
  • Opportunity Cost Variables: Supplies from divisions containing unused capacity carry an opportunity cost baseline of zero. Do not inflate minimum thresholds unless real market displacement occurs.
⚠️ ADVANCED EXAM TRAPS RECAP (INVISIBLE ERRORS):
  • The Fixed Cost Flexing Fault: Multiplied total fixed cost lines by actual volume indicators inside flexible summaries. Fixed items are strictly time-bound elements.
  • The Spare Capacity Illusion: Included market markup parameters in transfer minimum calculations when the source division has excess capacity. Opportunity cost remains zero.
  • The Raw Unit Margin Ranking Flaw: Evaluated restricted product options using absolute total contribution per product. You must divide the margin by bottleneck consumption per individual piece.
